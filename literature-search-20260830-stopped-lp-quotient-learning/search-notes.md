## Source-Supported Findings

1. Generic \(L_q\)-to-policy-regret margin powers already appear in ICML 2023.
2. Local-mass covariate-shift regression rates are already characterized.
3. Cross-fitted orthogonal nuisance learning and fast finite-model aggregation
   are established primitives.
4. Restricted conditional-mean OPE and KROPE occupy generic quotient/coverage
   and representation-stability claims.
5. Bellman error alone is not a reliable value-error surrogate.

## Inferences and Unknowns

- No inspected source states the full killed exponential first-passage,

- This absence is an inferred opportunity, not proof of novelty.
- A correct transformed baseline may learn or exploit the same quotient; a rate
  comparison with an ambient smoother does not establish universal superiority.
- The unresolved theorem is a learned-chart information/adaptation separation
  under matched source data.

## Handoff Notes

- Formula derivation should prove only a conditional stopped-risk estimator bound
  and explicitly retain chart-distortion, ratio, pseudooutcome, and transience
  terms.
- Idea optimization should not promote boundary weighting, cross-fitting, or
  finite candidate aggregation as standalone novelty.
- Experiment design should compare direct stopped-risk validation with Bellman
  loss and include correct risk-transformed Doob/DICE/KROPE baselines.
