# Idea-Grounding Packet

## Scope and Evidence Boundary

- Topic: cross-fitted quotient regression for an irreversible return-to-charge
  boundary under killed exponential Energy-to-Go.
- Source-supported: generic margin conversion, target-law regression,
  orthogonal nuisance learning, Q-aggregation, restricted OPE, and KROPE are
  occupied.
- Inferred gap: their composition with executed-interface first-passage risk and
  stopped stranding--throughput consequences was not located.
- Unknown: whether a matched-information learned Doob/KROPE estimator can attain
  exactly the same quotient rate.

## Minimum Viable Research Questions

1. Can a cross-fitted stopped-risk estimator attain target \(L_2\) rate governed
   by quotient local mass while exposing all nuisance and transience terms?
2. Can the quotient chart itself be learned at a rate that preserves this gain?
3. Is that learned-chart rate unavailable to a matched Doob/DICE/KROPE method,
   or is the honest result an equivalence plus a task-specific certificate?
