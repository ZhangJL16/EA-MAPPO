# Search Notes

## Safe Queries Used

- `Double Reinforcement Learning off-policy evaluation MDP JMLR`
- `marginalized density ratio doubly robust absorbing MDP stochastic shortest path`
- `risk-sensitive off-policy evaluation exponential utility MDP`
- `Off-Policy Risk Assessment in Markov Decision Processes`
- `semiparametric efficient OPE linear MDP`
- `off-environment policy evaluation two-stage density ratio`
- `doubly robust functionals product bias cross-fitting Biometrika`

Only public method names, titles, and problem keywords were queried; no private
draft text, local paths, or unpublished numerical results were sent.

## Sources Checked

- JMLR official paper pages
- PMLR official proceedings pages
- NeurIPS official proceedings pages
- Biometrika/Oxford Academic
- arXiv and DBLP for discovery or metadata cross-checks

## Excluded Sources

- MDPI search results were excluded by source policy.
- ResearchGate mirrors, theses, news releases, and untraceable PDFs were not used
  as final evidence when primary proceedings pages were available.
- Generic risk-sensitive control papers without OPE, transfer, or statistical
  estimation content were screened out of the final table.

## Source-Supported Findings Versus Inference

- Source-supported: DRL has cross-fitting, double robustness, efficiency, and
  fourth-root nuisance-rate conditions; OPRA-MDP has a DR CDF estimator and
  matching-order minimax risk bounds; Duan et al. use restricted chi-square
  divergence and CME/FQI equivalence; Katdare et al. factor off-environment ratios.
- Inference: none of the screened primary pages advertises the exact joint object
  of random charger hitting, killed Feynman--Kac occupation, known target safety
  composition, quotient support, and an irreversible-boundary decision lower
  bound. This is an opportunity hypothesis, not a novelty verdict.

## Unknowns

- Whether a stochastic-control or applied-probability paper outside the indexed
  OPE vocabulary already derives the canonical gradient for killed
  Feynman--Kac functionals.
- Whether OPRA-MDP's recursive DR-CDF estimator reduces exactly to the proposed
  stopped-MGF score after augmenting state by cumulative resource and stopping.
- Whether the two-layer score is the canonical gradient or only an unbiased,
  variance-suboptimal augmentation under the project's observed-data model.

## Handoff Notes

- For formula derivation: derive the pathwise derivative and tangent-space
  projection before designing another loss.
- For idea optimization: the rescue target is an efficiency/phase-transition
  theorem, not “DR plus risk weighting.”
- For experiment design: OPRA-DR, DRL/MWL-MQL, DualDICE/CoinDICE, direct
  exponential TD, and primitive plug-in are mandatory comparisons after Gates.
