## Corrections to the proposed diagnostic

Finite continuation improvement at a single prior cannot ensure minimax strictness. The exact requirement is improvement at every old least-favorable prior. A convenient sufficient certificate avoids locating them: any old public-prior Bayes lower value l and one new policy with worst risk U < l suffice. The lower prior is not labeled least-favorable.

Likewise, “asymptotic geometry unchanged” is not the conclusion. The maximum stays fixed because an old maximizing hypothesis's optimal dual survives; the A hypothesis coefficient decreases. The new bundled dual row can be tight, not inactive.

The proposed broad title “feedback geometry determines finite-budget risk” would overstate the result. Feedback protocols matter in addition to reward laws, durations, hypothesis class, and comparator. The updated title retains the actual existence result and its qualifications.

## Handwritten audit of the main argument

1. **Policy class.** The lower problem permits all supported low-capacity primitive feedback choices, including partially completed terminal sorties. At capacity three, after preparation there are two units; after one inspection only one remains, so a second inspection cannot preserve the required return reserve. The full low recursion includes immediate empty return and both inspection choices. The new upper policy commits to complete routes, a legal subset of the high policy class.
2. **Dock feedback.** Its law is identical across hypotheses. Omitting it from posterior state loses no Bayes information; any extra randomization based on such feedback cannot improve over a Bayesian best response, and the evaluated witness may legally ignore it.
3. **Bayes lower.** A fixed public prior gives a lower bound on worst-case regret by finite minimax; it does not choose a separate learner for each unknown truth.
4. **High upper.** Mixture weights are nonnegative and sum exactly to one. Each policy uses only the public model collection and its prior, not the true label. All allowed chosen routes fit the remaining calendar budget and finish at the regenerative state. A single mixture risk vector bounds high worst risk.
5. **Known-model terminal execution.** Any charger-terminal history decomposes into complete safe excursions. Expected realized reward equals the sum of chosen rows' expected rewards by the bounded martingale identity; feedback-dependent route completion does not invalidate this equality. Every complete excursion has nonnegative centered expected-row cost under the claimed gain. Thus terminal reward is at most T rho. Docking or repeated single-site optimal routes achieve it at T=12,24. This proof does not extend terminal equality to arbitrary positions or all budgets.
6. **Coefficient identities.** At q, A and AB both cost 3r-u and contribute the same q-versus-A KL; B adds zero KL for that constraint. A-only satisfies the other q alternative too. Under A, the confusing B alternative can be queried at centered cost v-r via B or v/3-r via AB. Under B, optimal B feedback distinguishes the alternatives at no suboptimal acquisition cost. The existing attainability theorem remains the dependency connecting these programs to attainable coefficients.
7. **Robustness.** Coupling every fixed policy with the same internal randomization gives a per-step observation mismatch bound epsilon until divergence. Reward difference summed over T is at most T(T+1)epsilon/2; comparator variation adds T epsilon. Taking infimum/max over the same policy mappings gives the policy-uniform risk Lipschitz constant T(T+3)/2. The perturbed witness need not stay Bayes optimal: its fixed mapping remains legal and has the same bound.
8. **Off-control.** All old primitive histories and laws remain the same and a second measurement is disallowed. Virtual-counter copying works both ways for the reachable operational protocol, giving exact risk equality; no new path-coverage penalty is inherent.
9. **Diagnostic proof.** Nonnegative continuous finite-game bottleneck terms on the compact prior simplex make the all-least-favorable coverage equivalence valid. At maximum level, an attained new dual with old maximal value also certifies an old optimum. Finite and attained optimization assumptions stay explicit.
10. **Transfer.** The screening-bench model copies preparation, individual feedback, and cleanup at primitive resolution, including arbitrary terminal positions. A path-catalogue label correspondence alone would not suffice for the full low-policy class; the provided history map supplies that correspondence.

No mathematical counterexample to the scoped main claim was found in this audit. This statement is limited to these arguments and computations, not a certificate of all earlier framework results.
