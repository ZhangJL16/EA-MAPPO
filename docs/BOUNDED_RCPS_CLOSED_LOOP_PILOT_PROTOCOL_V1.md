# Bounded RCPS Closed-Loop Pilot Protocol V1

## Status and scope

This protocol is frozen before any pilot outcome is inspected.  The pilot is a
mechanism and engineering validation, not publication evidence and not a new
RCPS certificate.  Its purpose is to test whether the independently confirmed
offline selective Gate transfers to adaptive closed-loop use without changing
the R3 navigation actor or its HOCBF safety layer.

The offline certificate concerns scene-grouped, forked-action samples.  It must
not be described as a worst-case guarantee or as a certificate for the adaptive
state distribution generated by this pilot.

## Cases and pairing

- 75 previously unused navigation cases: 15 in each of the five frozen R3
  distance buckets.
- Task seed: `830001`.
- Static-world seed: `840001 + case_index`.
- Budget-assignment seed: `850001`.
- Initial available-energy fractions: `0.20, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60`.
- Within each distance bucket, every budget occurs exactly three times.  The
  assignment is frozen by a deterministic seeded permutation.
- Each case is evaluated in three paired arms with identical task, obstacle
  layout, initial state, initial budget, actor, and HOCBF.

The three arms are:

1. `ungated`: execute the task, then return to the charger.
2. `geometry_gate`: evaluate the nominal R3 action with the frozen
   geometry-action critic and its RCPS threshold.
3. `certified_meet_gate`: evaluate the same nominal action with the pointwise
   minimum of the frozen geometry and direct critics and its RCPS threshold.

## Closed-loop intervention

At every policy step of the outbound task leg, a gated arm evaluates only the
nominal deterministic R3 action.  The available-budget input is

`initial_budget_fraction - cumulative_realized_energy / calibrated_capacity`.

If the score is at or above the arm's frozen threshold, the nominal action is
executed through the unchanged HOCBF.  If it is below threshold, the arm makes
one absorbing return commitment: the active goal becomes the charger, the
current position and velocity are preserved, and no later decision can resume
the task.  The Gate is not applied during the return leg.  Successful task
completion also retargets to the charger, preserving position and resetting
velocity to zero in accordance with the frozen forked-action data protocol.

Each leg has at most 4000 policy steps.  A rollout terminates on successful
return, collision, boundary contact, energy exhaustion, or environment timeout.
Energy exhaustion occurs when cumulative realized energy exceeds the initially
assigned available energy.  All realized energy is accounted for across both
legs.

## Outcomes

Primary paired outcomes are:

- task completion;
- safe return (charger reached without collision, boundary contact, or energy
  exhaustion);
- mission success (task completion and safe return);
- unsafe terminal event.

Secondary outcomes are task progress at termination/commitment, early-return
frequency and timing, energy used, total steps, Gate acceptance rate, HOCBF
intervention rate, and Gate inference latency.  Results are reported per arm,
per distance bucket, per budget level, and as paired Meet-minus-geometry and
Meet-minus-ungated differences with 10,000 case-bootstrap draws.

No run, arm, budget, or case may be removed after outcomes are observed.  A
crash may be resumed only from already atomically completed rollout records.

## Predeclared promotion Gate

The pilot is promoted to a formal closed-loop evaluation only if all conditions
hold:

1. All 225 paired rollouts complete and all frozen provenance checks pass.
2. The Meet intervention is non-degenerate: its early-return fraction lies in
   `[0.05, 0.95]`.
3. Every Meet rollout has Gate-inference p95 below one policy period (`0.2 s`).
4. Meet safe return is no more than one case (`1/75`) below geometry-only.
5. Meet has no more unsafe terminal events than geometry-only.
6. Meet task-completion rate is not below geometry-only.
7. Meet improves over geometry-only by at least one completed task (`1/75`) or
   by at least `0.01` in mean terminal task progress.
8. Meet improves safety over ungated by at least one case, measured as either
   one additional safe return or one fewer unsafe terminal event.

Failure of any condition means `DO_NOT_PROMOTE`; it is a diagnostic result, not
permission to tune on these 75 cases.  Any subsequent method change requires a
new protocol and fresh seeds.

## Smoke tests

Reduced smoke runs may use fewer cases or censored step limits only to validate
the execution path.  They are explicitly non-evidential and cannot be included
in the 75-case result.
