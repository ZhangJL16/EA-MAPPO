# Memory Route Result-to-Claim Gate

**Reviewer:** fresh same-family GPT-5.5 fallback, provisional

**Intended claim:** a physically structured recurrent memory observer yields a
tighter feedback-valid certified uncertainty set, reducing safety intervention
and energy while preserving collision safety.

## Verdict

`claim_supported: no`

`confidence: high`

## What the results support

The deterministic analytic interval observer supports conditional containment
and can feed a robust directional HOCBF under the declared association, trusted
base, bounded-noise/residual, sampled-data, and ideal-arithmetic assumptions.
Across the 12 controlled simulation scenarios, no method recorded a simulated
collision.

## What the results do not support

- Learned/Kalman/IMM development boxes are uncertified on every closed-loop
  step and have nonzero under-bound rates.
- The deterministic interval observer falls back/freezes on every step and has
  zero task success.
- The exact-state oracle still has `2.5204%` infeasible/uncertified fallback.
- Recurrent methods are neither the best estimator nor the tightest marginal
  q95 tube.
- There is no equal-deterministic-certification intervention or energy
  comparison.

## Revised defensible statement

> Under bounded-assumption simulation tests, a deterministic analytic interval
> observer can preserve containment and feed a robust HOCBF, but the evaluated
> learned/recurrent memory boxes do not provide feedback-valid deterministic
> certification or certified reductions in intervention or energy.

## Missing evidence

An independently valid recurrent-dependent radius, repeated-time coverage,
equal-certification comparisons, broader multi-seed scenarios, and certified
association/delay/dropout handling would be required to revisit the intended
claim. These are not treated as reasons to continue target mode because the
theorem object is already covered by closest prior work and every A–M family is
closed.
