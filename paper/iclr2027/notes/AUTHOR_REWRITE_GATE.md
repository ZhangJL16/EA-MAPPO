# Author Rewrite Gate for the Narrow AI Disclosure

The intended final disclosure is:

> Generative AI tools were used only to assist literature search, citation verification, and reference formatting. Generative AI tools were not used for other research or writing tasks in this submission. The authors reviewed the cited sources and bibliography and take responsibility for the final content.

This wording is **not yet valid for the current draft**. It becomes valid only after the authors independently replace, rather than merely copyedit, any AI-assisted non-literature material retained in the submission.

## Required author-owned replacement

- title, abstract, narrative framing, contribution list, and conclusions;
- problem formulation, theorem statements, mathematical claims, and proof text;
- method description, hypotheses, experimental design, baselines, gates, and statistical protocol;
- figure structure/content/caption and all non-result tables;
- manuscript prose, organization, and LaTeX source outside bibliography/reference formatting;
- final interpretation of code, artifacts, experiments, and results.

The authors may use the current draft and audits as a checklist of issues, but retaining their substantive wording, argument structure, mathematical formulation, or figure/table design would still be AI assistance that should be disclosed under the ICLR 2027 policy.

## Finalization check

1. Freeze the author-rewritten revision.
2. Confirm that AI was used only for literature search, citation verification, and reference formatting in that frozen revision.
3. Convert the paper statement from future tense to the intended past-tense disclosure above.
4. Rebuild and rerun citation, claim, anonymity, page, font, and metadata audits.
5. Use the same disclosure in the OpenReview submission form.
