# Visual Contract

## First-page overview

- Artifact: editable TikZ schematic (`figures/overview.tikz`).
- Core claim: return prediction must follow the executed policy--safety--plant chain and is evaluated at a one-way decision boundary.
- Reviewer question: what is predicted, what is safety-critical, and why do early/late decisions differ?
- Evidence layer: conceptual overview; no numerical evidence.
- Panel map: single pipeline plus directly labeled early/late consequences and safety-scope note.
- Palette: Okabe--Ito blue/orange/green/vermillion plus shape and labels; color is redundant.
- Caption role: self-contained system roles and non-certificate scope.
- Placement: first page, after the decision-chain paragraph.
- Traceability: vector source committed with manuscript; no generated or fabricated data.

## Result visuals

Main-frontier and mechanism figures are intentionally not rendered before data exist. Appendix result sheets define their source fields. No empty axes are used to imply trends.
