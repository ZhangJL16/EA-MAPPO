## Closest-work conclusion

The broad central claim—persistent task execution through energy-aware safety constraints and charging—is covered. The current project may still differentiate on discrete-time robust complete-support certification, independently frozen finite-rank recovery, exact publication-time authority bookkeeping, and pending-task identity across interruption. That is a narrow integration/specification delta, not yet a proved nonreducible principle.

## Rescue condition

Provide a concrete system and theorem showing why augmented-state CBF/LDCBF persistification cannot express the selected recovery-certificate rank, full-support uncertainty envelope, publication-time fail-closed distinction, or same-task identity condition; otherwise frame these as rigorous engineering/theory integration and rely on matched empirical benefit.
