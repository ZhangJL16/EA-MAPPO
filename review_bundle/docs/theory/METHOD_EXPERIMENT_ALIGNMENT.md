## Required causal controls

1. Dual fields: equal parameter count, equal total labels, equal horizons where possible, separate calibration, shared-versus-separate trunk, scalar fusion control, and privileged-information audit.
2. Teacher: no guidance, prefill only, actor warm start only, both; identical online steps, seeds, starts, SOC bins, rewards, and clean observations.
3. Generator: center, `G`, support volume, entropy/temperature, fallback, replay action semantics, and observation differences varied one at a time.
4. Charging: 1×/2×, policy-only/system-with-kappa, initial SOC, hold, departure gate, reward energy scale, and teacher treatment separated.
