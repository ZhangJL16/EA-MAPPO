## Prior-Art Verdict Before Theorem Drafting

1. **Static non-identifiability is not a new theorem class.** A two-environment construction that differs only on rejected actions is exactly a zero-overlap observational-equivalence construction.
2. **Dynamic conservatism is not yet separated.** SafeMDP already states impossibility without structural regularity, defines iterative safe-set expansion, and characterizes the maximum safely reachable region. Liaw and Plaut add an abstention/impossibility result for catastrophic feedback.
3. **Safe probing is directly occupied.** SafeOpt, SafeMDP, Wachi et al., and ActSafe choose informative evaluations or trajectories subject to safety constraints.
4. **Probe resource consumption is representable by existing state augmentation.** Budgeted MDPs and bandits with knapsacks already treat actions as consuming limited resources.
5. **Two-level censoring is descriptive, not yet theorem-level novelty.** An action filter plus an absorbing commitment action can be represented as one sequential policy with action-dependent feedback and structural zeros in its occupancy measure.
6. The only remaining chance for a new result would be a lower bound or identifiability separation that provably cannot be reduced to no-overlap, SafeMDP-style safe-set reachability, active safe identification, or resource-constrained exploration. No such separation has been found in this attack.

## Search Limits

- This is a strongest-effort primary-source attack, not a proof that no related paper exists.
- Recent 2025–2026 preprints were included where they directly attack the candidate; their publication status is not treated as peer-review validation.
- Broad “shielded RL data bias” terminology is inconsistent across papers. The closest rigorous objects appear under safe exploration, conservative safety critics, partial monitoring, and performative feedback rather than that exact phrase.
