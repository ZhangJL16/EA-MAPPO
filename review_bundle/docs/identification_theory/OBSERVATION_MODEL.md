# Observation Model

## Full-Data Environment

Let $\mathcal{M}$ denote a controlled stochastic environment. At task time $t$:

- $S_t\in\mathcal{S}$ is the physical state;
- $E_t\in\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ is remaining battery;
- $Z_t\in\{\mathrm{TASK},\mathrm{CHARGER\_COMMITTED}\}$ is the mode;
- $A_t^P\in\mathcal{A}$ is the action proposed by the nominal task policy;
- $A_t^X$ is the physically executed action;
- $F_t\in\{0,1\}$ is the action-filter decision while $Z_t=\mathrm{TASK}$.

For each feasible task action $a$, define the one-step potential outcome

$$
Y_t(a)=\bigl(S_{t+1}(a),L_t^C(a),C_t^E(a)\bigr),
$$

where $L_t^C(a)$ is a collision or near-collision label and $C_t^E(a)\ge 0$ is physical energy consumption. Only the potential outcome associated with the executed action can be observed.

## Observed Filtration

Let $\mathcal{H}_t$ be the sigma-field generated by all logged variables strictly before the filter decision at time $t$, including previous proposals, filter decisions, executed actions, realized transitions, energy measurements, and modes. The proposal policy and filter may be adaptive:

$$
A_t^P\sim\pi_t^P(\cdot\mid \mathcal{H}_t,S_t),
$$

$$
F_t=f_t(\mathcal{H}_t,S_t,A_t^P,U_t^F),
$$

where $U_t^F$ represents logged or unlogged internal randomization. A deterministic filter is included as a special case.

The filter decision is known after it is made. Knowing $F_t=0$ does not reveal $Y_t(A_t^P)$.

## Commitment Stopping Time

Let

$$
\tau_c=\inf\{t\ge 0:G_t(\mathcal{H}_t,S_t,E_t)=1\}
$$

be the charger-commitment time. We require $\tau_c$ to be a stopping time with respect to the observed filtration enlarged by the current state and battery. Define

$$
M_t^T=\mathbf{1}\{t<\tau_c\}.
$$

Within a sortie, commitment is absorbing:

$$
Z_t=\mathrm{CHARGER\_COMMITTED}
\Longrightarrow
Z_{t+1}=\mathrm{CHARGER\_COMMITTED}
$$

until charging completes.

## Action-Level Observation Indicator

The user-level shorthand $M_t^A=F_t$ is valid only while task mode is active. For full bookkeeping define

$$
M_t^A=M_t^T F_t.
$$

Thus a proposed task-action outcome is observed exactly when the system has not committed and the filter permits the proposal.

## Execution Rule

Before commitment,

$$
A_t^X=
\begin{cases}
A_t^P, & F_t=1,\\
B_t, & F_t=0,
\end{cases}
$$

where $B_t$ is the actually executed safe alternative, hold action, or filter-selected replacement. After commitment, $A_t^X$ is drawn from the charger-goal behavior. The model does not assume that a rejected proposal has the same outcome as $B_t$.

## Observed Dataset

The logged data are

$$
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{obs}}=
\left\{
S_t,E_t,Z_t,A_t^P,A_t^X,F_t,M_t^T,
Y_t(A_t^X),\text{ filter/commitment metadata}
\right\}_{t\ge 0}.
$$

When $M_t^A=0$, $Y_t(A_t^P)$ is absent unless $A_t^P=A_t^X$ for an independently justified reason. When $M_t^T=0$, the charger trajectory may remain observed, but the potential suffix under continued task behavior is absent.

## Full-Data Versus Observed Law

Let $P_{\mathcal{M}}^{\mathrm{full}}$ be the law of all potential outcomes under environment $\mathcal{M}$, and let

$$
P_{\mathcal{M}}^{\mathrm{obs}}
=
\mathsf{Obs}_{\pi^P,F,G}\left(P_{\mathcal{M}}^{\mathrm{full}}\right)
$$

be its pushforward through the proposal, filter, execution, and commitment mechanism. Identification of a target $\psi(\mathcal{M})$ requires

$$
P_{\mathcal{M}_1}^{\mathrm{obs}}
=P_{\mathcal{M}_2}^{\mathrm{obs}}
\Longrightarrow
\psi(\mathcal{M}_1)=\psi(\mathcal{M}_2)
$$

for every pair in the declared model class.

## Reduction to an Augmented MDP

Let $X_t=(S_t,E_t,Z_t,\Xi_t)$, where $\Xi_t$ contains all learner/filter state needed for Markovity. Let the augmented action include the proposal and intervention choice. Charger commitment is an absorbing mode transition. Under this augmentation, the observed process is a standard sequential behavior policy with an action-dependent feedback map. Rejected actions and post-commitment task actions have zero occupancy.

This reduction is central: two indicator variables do not by themselves define a new identification theory. A new result must rely on structure not preserved by this augmented-MDP/no-overlap representation.
