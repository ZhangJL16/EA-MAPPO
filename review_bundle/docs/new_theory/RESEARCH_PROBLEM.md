# Data-Driven Dual-Timescale Safety for Persistent UAVs

## Target

Study whether a persistent UAV can learn, from repeated flight data, an action-level collision hazard and a **risk-budget-conditioned charger-continuation distribution** that jointly determine immediate action admission and one-way task termination.

## Status

COHERENT AFTER PROBLEM REFORMULATION; RESEARCH DIRECTION BLOCKED ON ICLR-LEVEL NOVELTY.

The former invariant pair `(U_C(s,a), U_E(s,a))` was still an obvious product construction. The canonical invariant is now the **collision-budget-conditioned defective energy-return law** generated by the actual collision-filtered charger policy. The old pair is retained only as a degenerate special case when the continuation policy does not depend on collision admissibility or remaining risk.

## Invariant Object

Let `x_t` be the Markov state or sufficient observable history, and let `b_t` be the remaining sortie collision-risk budget. A predictable risk spend `d_t` must satisfy

`0 <= d_t <= b_t`, `U_C^1(x_t,a_t) <= d_t`, and `b_{t+1}=b_t-d_t`.

The charger uses the same base goal-conditioned policy `pi_theta(.|x,g_c)`, restricted by the learned collision envelope and remaining budget. Denote the resulting deployment kernel by

`kappa_{C,eta}(a,d | x,b)`.

It is a filter/renormalization of the charger-goal policy, not a recovery controller. Its version `eta` identifies the collision model, calibration set, selector, allocation rule, and base-policy checkpoint.

For one candidate `(a,d)`, define the extended random return

`Z_tilde_E^eta(x,a,d,b) in [0,+infinity]`:

- finite and equal to cumulative telemetry energy if the risk-budgeted charger continuation reaches the charger before collision or selector failure;
- `+infinity` if collision occurs, the admissible support becomes empty, or the charger is never reached.

Equivalently, the finite part is a defective CDF

`F_E^eta(z | x,a,d,b) = P(Z_tilde_E^eta <= z)`.

Its missing finite mass is the probability of collision, empty-support failure, or non-arrival. This object prevents the energy critic from silently conditioning away precisely the trajectories that invalidate return-to-charger feasibility.

## Core Scientific Question

Can repeated sorties learn and calibrate the budget-indexed law `F_E^eta`, when its target distribution is endogenous to a learned collision filter, well enough to decide:

1. whether a current action's swept transition can be admitted;
2. whether a collision-budget-respecting charger continuation still has a finite, sufficiently tight energy upper tail;
3. when continuing the task would leave no admissible charger-continuation envelope and therefore trigger irreversible charger commitment?

## Why This Is Not the Previous Product Construction

The energy target is not `Z_E(s,a)` learned independently of collision. It is

`Z_tilde_E^eta(x,a,d,b)`,

whose law changes when any of the following changes: remaining risk budget, collision calibration, candidate selector, allocation rule, or charger-policy checkpoint. A state-action-only energy critic cannot represent this family in general. `COUPLED_SAFETY_OPERATOR.md` gives an exact counterexample and a stability condition.

The formulation is nevertheless representable as an augmented-state chance-constrained SSP. That general representability is **not** claimed as novel. The candidate contribution is the learning problem created by an **estimated, selection-valid collision envelope inducing the target distribution of a calibrated distributional energy critic**, together with explicit failure mass and one-way stopping.

## Research Questions

1. Can the defective return law and its missing mass be learned without survivor bias from completed charger returns?
2. Does conditioning on `b` and collision-filter version `eta` matter relative to a collision-independent energy critic?
3. How sensitive is the return target to small changes in calibrated collision risk near admission boundaries?
4. Can calibration remain valid after candidate selection and repeated policy/filter updates, or must claims suspend under detected shift?
5. Does a two-resource stopping boundary improve the measurable operational envelope relative to matched CMDP, CVaR, distance, scalar-TD, and independent-critic baselines?

6. When a learned collision filter changes, can admission-boundary visitation quantify how much the charger-return law and its calibrated coverage drift, thereby deciding whether old return data remain reusable or must be invalidated?

## Primary Theoretical Contribution Candidate After Redesign 2

The strongest current candidate is no longer the budgeted Bellman operator. It is the **filter-induced target-transport problem**:

- C2 proves arbitrarily small collision-bound changes can create order-one energy-target jumps at admission boundaries;
- C4 bounds return-law drift by cumulative induced-kernel drift;
- C5 specializes that bound to collision-admission boundary visitation;
- C6 converts the return-law drift bound into an explicit calibration-coverage debit;
- the resulting transport-or-recalibrate gate is testable against unconditional freezing, naive replay reuse, and full recollection.

This contribution remains vulnerable to generic Markov-kernel perturbation and off-policy/conformal transport prior art. It is not accepted until a new closest-work attack and hostile panel pass.

## Final Reformulation — Commitment-Censored Supervision

The most fundamental data question is now explicit: a task action has a charger-return potential outcome, but ordinary flight logs reveal that outcome only when the system actually commits immediately afterward. Without commitment positivity and ignorability, the desired action-conditioned distribution is not identifiable from routine sorties.

`COMMITMENT_CENSORED_IDENTIFICATION.md` proves non-identification, gives a randomized-probe identification formula, and derives a finite-grid `1/sqrt(Np)` supervision rate. This replaces the unsupported phrase “repeated sorties provide self-generated supervision” with a precise requirement: repeated sorties provide such supervision only through logged, version-matched commitment probes or another justified off-policy design.

The resulting primary learning object remains `F_c(z|W)`, but the primary learning problem is now **selectively observed counterfactual charger-return estimation under a throughput-constrained probe policy**.
