## Current Research Question

Can a persistent UAV learn from repeated flight data both an immediate selected-action collision hazard and a collision-filter-induced charger-return distribution, while correctly handling remaining risk budget, failed/non-arriving returns, policy/filter drift, selectively observed charger-return labels, and irreversible task stopping?

2. sequential swept-collision risk spending for variable-length sorties;
3. filter-version target transport bounds C4--C6;

## Primary Learning Object

`F_c(z|W)`, the defective distribution of post-action telemetry energy under immediate charger commitment, where `W=(x,a,d,b,eta)` records state/history, action, risk spend, remaining collision budget, and collision-filter/policy version. Infinite/missing mass represents collision, empty support, or non-arrival.

## Empirical Status

An artifact-level audit on 2026-08-13 found that E1 did not complete. Seeds 0/1/2 have no live process, no `COMPLETED.json`, no estimator checkpoint, and no result summary. Seed 0 contains only 43/100 raw charger-return trajectories; seeds 1 and 2 contain none. All three are marked `INVALID_FOR_FORMAL_COMPARISON`.

The interrupted seed-0 data are diagnostic only: mean return energy is 0.4735, mean successor distance is 2.2718 m, and energy–distance correlation is 0.9603. Initial SOC is exactly 1.0 for all 43 trajectories and mean final SOC is 0.9995265 because the protocol inherited the navigation baseline's 1000-unit nonterminating budget.

The current E1 protocol also commits to the charger after one task action and implements no fixed-SOC, distance, learned, or calibrated switching comparison. Consequently TD advantage, distributional-tail advantage, calibration, conservatism, and switching performance are all unresolved.
