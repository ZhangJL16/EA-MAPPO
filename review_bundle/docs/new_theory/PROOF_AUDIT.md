## Correction to Sequential-Risk Notation

Let `L` be the charger/timeout decision horizon that would stop the process absent collision, and let actual execution stop at `min(L,T_B)`. The event of interest is `T_B<=L`. T5 sums predictable spends only while alive, `t<min(L,T_B)`. This avoids the ambiguous expression “collision before a stopping time that already includes collision.”
