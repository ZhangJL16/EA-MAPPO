# Blind Review Packet — Round 1

## Claimed Problem

A persistent UAV uses an action filter and a one-way charger commitment. Rejected actions reveal no physical outcomes; commitment removes the task-continuation suffix. The paper candidate asks whether the two mechanisms create a new identification problem.

## Available Technical Results

1. Formal observed-data law with proposal, executed action, filter, and stopping time.
2. Two-environment construction for a never-executed action.
3. Two-environment construction for a never-observed continuation suffix.
4. Augmented-MDP reduction in which both mechanisms generate zero occupancy.

## Candidate Claim

“Safety-induced two-level censoring makes parts of the safety boundary non-identifiable and motivates safe information acquisition under collision and energy budgets.”

## Review Instruction

Judge theory, novelty, and methodology independently. Treat the UAV application as non-novel unless the information structure or theorem resists reduction to existing frameworks.
