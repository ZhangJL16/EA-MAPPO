# Divergence-onset audit v1

Retrospective archived-only audit of all 41 same-arrival, joint-matched divergent pairs from retained-counterpart v1 (29 roots). No simulations, additional pairs, policy changes, fitted predictors, or causal onset claims.

Two alignments: downstream decision ordinal (root forced action excluded, compare kind/task ID, not reason) and common absolute-time event grid. Preserve unequal sequence lengths as unmatched, not invented actions. Normalize stored times to original 0.05-second physical grid only after <=1e-6 roundoff check; simultaneous events from both branches form one batch.

Report: first downstream action-identity mismatch; first corresponding decision-time mismatch; first recharge request time difference, first charger-arrival and charge-complete time difference, first completed charging-duration difference (missing/censored distinct); first exact queue-ID mismatch and first queue mismatch after removing the pair's two original IDs, including a post-both-first-completed landmark; first arrival acceptance mismatch and first cumulative overflow-count mismatch.

Throughput timeline: cumulative completed counts from root, signed i-j. First nonzero difference is usually a trivial first-task phase effect. Also report first difference after both first tasks finish; onset of final-winner persistent lead (earliest completion-event batch after which sign never returns to zero or reverses before W); onset of final exact count gap (D remains final D). These are retrospective endpoint-defined summaries, not the time an action causally determined the outcome. Count downstream decisions selected strictly before the onset event batch (exclude a new decision at that completion timestamp); also retain through-batch counts and both sides separately.

Compare completed task ID sets (not just counts) before/after onset and record whether they ever became equal and nonempty after both first completions. Reconstruct pending queue IDs from actual top-level decisions and accepted arrivals, check every logged predecision queue, never include nested oracle events. State context: exact reconstructed queue/completed IDs plus most recent archived predecision observation, its timestamp and age; battery/position at unlogged simultaneous times remain unavailable, do not interpolate.

Publish per-pair event timelines with task/return/charge/arrival/overflow/wait records and an offline HTML viewer. Keep all pair summaries and source/battery stratification. Root and pair dependence remains; no IID significance tests. Report onset association with prior recharge/overflow, not mutually exclusive mechanism classes A-D. Seeing early divergence does not prove short lookahead suffices; seeing late settled gap does not prove long-horizon planning is necessary.
